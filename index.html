Common questions

Good decisions start with clear answers.

Have a different question? Call our team at (276) 690-8331.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotes for lawn care, landscape installation, and hardscape projects. Tell us a little about the property and we’ll arrange the right next step.

Which areas do you serve?

Our core service area includes Wise, Norton, Abingdon, Big Stone Gap, and Lebanon in Virginia, along with Kingsport, Bristol, and Johnson City in Tennessee. Contact us if you are nearby.

Do you work with commercial properties?

Yes. We serve both residential and commercial properties with recurring lawn maintenance, landscape improvements, cleanup work, and custom projects.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. McGuire’s Lawn & Landscaping is licensed and insured, helping protect your property and giving you confidence throughout the project.

What happens after I request a quote?

We’ll review your request, contact you to clarify the scope, and arrange an on-site assessment when needed. Most initial responses are provided within one business day.
