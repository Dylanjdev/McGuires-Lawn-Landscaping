Common questions about our services

Do you offer free quotes? ▼

Yes! We provide free, no-obligation quotes for all services. Contact us by phone, email, or through our form and we'll schedule a time to assess your property.

What areas do you serve? ▼

We proudly serve Southwest Virginia and Tennessee including Wise VA, Abingdon VA, Norton VA, Big Stone Gap VA, Lebanon VA, Kingsport TN, Bristol TN, Johnson City TN, and surrounding areas in both states. Not sure if we cover your location? Just ask!

Are you licensed and insured? ▼

Absolutely. McGuire's Lawn & Landscaping is fully licensed and insured to protect both our team and your property.

How often should I schedule lawn care? ▼

Most residential lawns benefit from weekly or bi-weekly mowing during the growing season. We'll help you determine the best schedule based on your grass type and property needs.

Do you do both residential and commercial properties? ▼

Yes! We serve both residential homeowners and commercial businesses. No property is too big or too small.

We accept cash, check, and most major credit cards. Payment is typically due upon completion of service or based on the terms outlined in your service agreement.
